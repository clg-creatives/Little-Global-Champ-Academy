Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Little Global Champ.

What ages does Little Global Champ serve?
We serve children aged 2 to 5 years old. Our curriculum is developmentally appropriate for each age group.
Do you accept children with special needs?
Yes! We are an inclusive center under R.A. 11650. We welcome children with ASD Level 1, GDD, ADHD, speech delay, and mild Down Syndrome with reasonable accommodations.
What makes Little Global Champ different from other preschools?
We are the only preschool in DMCI that explicitly teaches 8 pillars: English, GMRC, Culture, Life Skills, Ethics, Confidence, Integrity, and Safety Drills (Turtle Pose, Firefighter Crawl, Hide & Hush).
Is Little Global Champ a registered entity?
Yes. ACHIE EARLY CHILD DEVELOPMENT CRADLE CO. is a General Partnership registered with the SEC under No. SEC260516-TR5ARDEFZWCHELE (currently under review).
Where are your classrooms located?
Our headquarters is at 1206 Trade and Finance Tower, BGC. Our classrooms are inside DMCI estates: Acacia Estates, Sheridan, Tree Park, Princeton, Creek Side, and more coming soon.
Can the Partnership accept donations?
Yes. Our Articles of Partnership expressly authorize us to receive donations, sponsorships, grants, and endowments – local and foreign. Official donation receipts are issued.
What safety drills do you teach?
Turtle Pose (earthquake), Firefighter Crawl (fire), Hide & Hush (lockdown), and Safety Selfie after every drill.
How do I enroll my child?
Click the "Enroll Now" button, fill out the form, and we will contact you within 24 hours to schedule an assessment and tour.
