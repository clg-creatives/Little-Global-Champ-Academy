Our Story

From a Sunday school teacher to a SEC-registered General Partnership serving children with special needs

ACHIE EARLY CHILD DEVELOPMENT CRADLE CO.

Little Global Champ Child Development Academy is operated by ACHIE EARLY CHILD DEVELOPMENT CRADLE CO., a General Partnership registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) under Registration No. SEC260516-TR5ARDEFZWCHELE (currently under review).

The Academy was founded by Teacher Achie (Marjorie Arenas ), a passionate educator who saw the gap: preschools teach ABCs, but no one teaches confidence, integrity, good manners, and safety drills – explicitly. With a background in online English teaching and Sunday school values formation, Teacher Achie built a center that nurtures the whole child.

The Partnership is expressly authorized under its Articles of Partnership to receive and solicit sponsorships, donations, grants, and endowments – both local and foreign – to support its premium inclusive education services for children with special needs.

We are fully compliant with Republic Act No. 12199 (ECCD System Act of 2025) and Republic Act No. 11650 (Inclusive Education Act).

Legal Status: ACHIE EARLY CHILD DEVELOPMENT CRADLE CO. is a General Partnership. Each Partner is a General Partner and may be personally liable for partnership debts as provided by law. The Partnership has posted a surety bond as required by SEC Memorandum Circular No. 7, Series of 2019.

Our Compass

Mission

To nurture confident, honest, and safe children through our 8-pillar curriculum, with specialized support for children with special needs, delivered in accessible locations within DMCI estates and clustered communities, operating as a transparent and accountable General Partnership.

Vision

A Philippines where every child aged 2-5, regardless of ability, has access to quality early childhood education that builds character, confidence, and safety awareness, supported by a sustainable partnership model.

Core Values (I.C.E.S.)

Inclusion – Every child belongs, with reasonable accommodations

Empowerment – Children keep themselves safe
Service – Partnership committed to community

Our 8 Pillars of Holistic Development

What makes Little Global Champ different? We teach what other preschools don't.
📖

English & Literacy

Letter sounds, vocabulary, conversation, and pre-writing skills through songs and stories.

🙏

GMRC (Good Manners)

"Po," "opo," please, thank you, excuse me, mano po, sharing, and gentle hands.

🌍

Cultural Exposure

Philippine festivals (Sinulog, Ati-Atihan) and global celebrations (Lunar New Year, Diwali).

🧼

Life Skills

Table manners, 8-step toilet independence, and handwashing song.

⚖️

Ethics

Fairness, honesty, kindness – taught through parables and real-life scenarios.

💪

Confidence Building

Mirror self-portraits, emotion check-in, "I Can't YET" growth mindset.

🔐

Integrity Development

Truth vs. lie, honest apologies, promise chain, integrity pledge.

🛡️

Safety & Emergency

Turtle Pose (earthquake), Firefighter Crawl, Hide & Hush, Safety Selfie.

Inclusive Education for All

Under R.A. 11650 and R.A. 12199, we provide reasonable accommodations for children with special needs.

We Welcome Children With:

  • Autism Spectrum Disorder (Level 1)
  • Global Developmental Delay (mild-moderate)
  • ADHD (with shadow if recommended)
  • Speech and Language Delay
  • Down Syndrome (mild, with shadow)

Our Inclusive Services:

  • Individualized Inclusion Plan (IIP)
  • Individualized Safety Plan (ISP)
  • Shadow Teacher integration
  • Visual schedules and PECS
  • Sensory breaks and Safe Corner

🛡️ Safety Without Fear

The only preschool in DMCI with explicit, age-appropriate safety drills.
🐢

Turtle Pose

Earthquake response: Drop, Cover, Hold. Taught through play, not fear.

🔥

Firefighter Crawl

Stop, Drop, Crawl like a snake. Low smoke, low crawl.

🤫

Hide & Hush

Lockdown response: Hide in safe corner, be silent like mice.

📸

Safety Selfie

After every drill, we take a group photo. "I am safe. I am brave."
