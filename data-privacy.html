Data Privacy Notice

Pursuant to Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

Data Controller

ACHIE EARLY CHILD DEVELOPMENT CRADLE CO., operating as Little Global Champ Child Development Academy, is the data controller for personal information collected through our website and enrollment processes.

Personal Information Collected

We collect: names, contact details, child's age and developmental information, payment information, and special needs disclosures.

Purpose of Collection

Information is collected for: enrollment processing, communication with parents, compliance with regulatory requirements (DSWD, ECCD Council, LGU), and donation/sponsorship management.

Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on: consent (for marketing), contract (for enrollment), and legal obligation (for regulatory compliance).

Data Sharing

We share data only with: government regulators (as required by law), payment processors, and our General Partners. We do not sell personal data.

Data Retention

Enrollment records: 5 years after child graduates. Financial records: 10 years. Donation records: 10 years.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase, and object to processing of your personal data. Contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@childev-cradle.org.

Data Breach Protocol

In case of a data breach, we will notify affected individuals and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours as required by law.
