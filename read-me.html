Read Me

Transparency, legal structure, and operational framework.

Who We Are

ACHIE EARLY CHILD DEVELOPMENT CRADLE CO. (General Partnership) operating as Little Global Champ Child Development Academy. A registered Child Development Center under R.A. 12199.

Our Mission

To nurture confident, honest, and safe children through our 8-pillar curriculum, with specialized support for children with special needs, delivered in accessible locations within DMCI estates and clustered communities.

Legal Authority

Under our Articles of Partnership (SEC Reg. No. SEC260516-TR5ARDEFZWCHELE, Under Review), we are authorized to:

  • Operate a Child Development Center under R.A. 12199
  • Provide inclusive education for children with special needs under R.A. 11650
  • Solicit, receive, and administer donations, sponsorships, grants, endowments – local and foreign
  • Borrow, mortgage, hypothecate, and encumber assets
  • Enter into joint ventures, partnerships, and collaborations
  • Establish branches locally and overseas
